STEP 1: READ THE INSTRUCTIONS

Genuine or Counterfeit?

In each trial, you will see {{ n }} product photos from five different brands (Adidas, Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Nike, Puma), decide whether they are Genuine or Counterfeit and rate your confidence.

Counterfeits can mislead shoppers, so we study whether people can make better authenticity judgments with AI than either can alone.

GenuineOriginally made by the stated brand.

CounterfeitAn imitation presented as that brand.

What to do

  1. Inspect the photo, including the logo, stitching, materials, and overall construction.
  2. Choose Genuine or Counterfeit and rate your confidence{% if condition == 'ai' %} to save your independent answer{% endif %}.
    3. {% if condition == 'ai' %}
  3. Review the AI prediction, confidence, and heatmap. Keep or change your answer, rate your final confidence and trust in the AI, and select at least one reason for your decision.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. Submit and move to the next photo.
  5. After all {{ n }} photos, answer a short question about mental effort {% if condition == 'ai' %} and AI usefulness{% endif %} during the tasks.

    6. If uncertain, give your best judgment and mark “I’m not sure”; you can also add a note or report an issue. Please use only the photo{% if condition == 'ai' %} and the AI assistance provided here{% endif %}, without outside searches or other AI tools.

What's different when using AI

Check product details and image context that the AI may overlook{% if condition == 'ai' %}, and whether its heatmap highlights relevant evidence{% endif %}. Use your judgment to catch mistakes{% if condition == 'ai' %} and reject misleading recommendations{% endif %}.

How humans can help

Use your understanding of logos, materials, stitching, and context to check evidence and spot AI mistakes. In With AI, decide whether the heatmap focuses on useful product details or irrelevant regions, and override a misleading recommendation when appropriate. Your judgments and notes help researchers evaluate human–AI collaboration. The model is fixed and does not retrain during the task.

Genuine

A product manufactured by the stated brand. Consistent logos, stitching, and materials may support this judgment.

Counterfeit

A product imitating the stated brand. Unusual logo shape, inconsistent construction, or poor finishing may be clues.

Example: An uneven seam or distorted wordmark may suggest a counterfeit. A clean logo alone is not proof of a genuine product. These clues are imperfect; judge all visible evidence together.
